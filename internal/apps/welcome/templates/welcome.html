TOMB Cares

Mental health awareness · Community support · Resources

{{.Guild}} has always been about more than games, progression and achievements. Behind every username is a real person with their own goals, challenges, victories and difficult moments.

TOMB Cares is an initiative focused on mental health awareness, emotional well-being, and connecting members of our community with reliable resources when they need support. Our goal is simple: no one should feel they have to navigate a difficult moment completely alone.

What TOMB Cares is

  • Mental health awareness and education
  • Access to reputable support resources
  • A community where asking for help is not treated as weakness
  • Encouragement for healthy communication and boundaries
  • Guidance toward professional support when appropriate
  • A culture built around compassion and respect

Sometimes someone needs advice. Sometimes they need someone willing to listen. And sometimes the best thing we can do is help them find someone professionally equipped to help.

What TOMB Cares is not

  • TOMB Cares is not therapy or professional mental-health treatment.
  • TOMB staff and community members are not expected to diagnose conditions, provide medical advice, recommend treatments, or become responsible for another person's mental health.

Our responsibility is much simpler:

Listen. Support. Connect.

We can care about someone without pretending we are qualified to treat them.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line right now. In the US and Canada, call or text 988. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential lines by country.

Names on this page are characters, not people: the same public information Blizzard's Armory shows about any guild. Nothing about a member's account appears here.
