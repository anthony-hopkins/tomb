Combat logs

Upload the game's combat log from a raid night and the site reads it into your pulls: each boss, kill or wipe, and your own numbers on every one. The file itself is deleted the moment it has been read; upload it again if you need it read again.

A combat log contains everyone who was in the raid. Only your own characters are kept; nothing about anyone else is. Switch on Advanced Combat Logging (Options → Network) before you raid, or the log will not record your gear and talents at each pull. Clear WoWCombatLog.txt between nights; the site accepts up to {{.Limit}} compressed.

This page needs a current browser to upload.
